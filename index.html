About the game

Mining Clicker is an idle/clicker game. You are a miner digging for gold: click the rock, collect gold and use it to grow your mining empire.

How to play

  1. Click the big rock to mine gold.
  2. Buy a better pickaxe to get more gold from every click.
  3. Hire miners to collect gold automatically every second.
  4. See your progress in the statistics section.
  5. Progress saves automatically. Use Reset Game to start over.
